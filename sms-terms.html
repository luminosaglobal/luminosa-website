Program description

Luminosa Global Inc. ("Luminosa Global," "we," "us") operates an SMS messaging program for people who have requested information about Italian citizenship recognition services. Once you consent, we send text messages containing your consultation booking link, follow-up reminders about scheduling your consultation, and responses to questions you send us.

How you opt in

You are added to this program only when you affirmatively consent. Consent is collected through the lead form on our advertisements and website, where you enter your mobile number and check a separate, unchecked box authorizing Luminosa Global to text you. You may review the exact consent language and form at luminosa.global/sms-opt-in.html.

Consent is never a condition of purchase. You do not have to agree to receive text messages in order to buy any product or service from Luminosa Global. Declining SMS does not affect your consultation or your case.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Under normal operation you will receive up to 3 messages after submitting your information, plus any replies to messages you send us.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Luminosa Global does not charge for these messages, but your mobile carrier's standard messaging and data charges apply to every message sent and received.

How to stop messages

Reply STOP to any message to cancel. After you send STOP we will send one confirmation message and then stop texting you. You can also reply END, QUIT, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, or REVOKE. To rejoin, submit the form again or reply START.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance. You can also email drmeschino@luminosa.global or call us at +1 (312) 217-6730.

Supported carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is subject to effective transmission from your wireless service provider and is outside our control.

Your privacy

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes. Your mobile information is used only to operate this messaging program and is shared only with the messaging service providers that transmit our texts on our behalf, and only for that purpose.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Eligibility and accuracy

You must be at least 18 years old and the authorized subscriber or account holder of the mobile number you provide. If your number changes or is reassigned, notify us immediately at drmeschino@luminosa.global.
