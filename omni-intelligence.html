How to Read Omni Intelligence

Each profile is compiled from publicly reported decisions and community discussions among applicants and practitioners. Predictability ratings reflect how consistent a court's reported outcomes appear — not the strength of any individual case. There is no single "Italian court approach": different courts, and different judges, may interpret the same legal questions differently, and appeals and Constitutional Court developments continue to reshape the landscape.

No reported decision guarantees a similar outcome. Every case depends on its own facts, evidence, filing date, and legal arguments. This page is educational information, not legal advice — always review current developments with a qualified attorney.
