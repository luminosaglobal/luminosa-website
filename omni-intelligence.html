How This Research Works

Each profile is compiled from publicly reported decisions and community discussions among applicants and practitioners. Predictability ratings express how consistent a court's reported outcomes appear — they are probability signals, not the strength of any individual case. There is no single "Italian court approach": different courts, and different judges, may interpret the same legal questions differently, and appeals and Constitutional Court developments continue to reshape the landscape.

This is research, not legal advice. Omni Intelligence is compiled by Domenico of Luminosa Global — a researcher, not an attorney — and no reported decision guarantees a similar outcome; every case depends on its own facts, evidence, filing date, and legal arguments. Luminosa Global operates in partnership with an attorney; strategy for your specific case is developed in consultation, never from a webpage.
